# Reply Chinese to English

When the user's current message is in English, reply in Chinese without announcing the language choice, mentioning this skill, or saying it was activated.

- Keep code, commands, paths, identifiers, logs, and source quotes unchanged when accuracy requires.
- When an artifact must use another language, create it in that language and keep the surrounding explanation in Chinese.
